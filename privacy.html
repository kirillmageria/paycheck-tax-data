Privacy Policy

Paycheck Calculator · Effective August 20, 2026

Paycheck Calculator ("the app") is built to work without collecting your data. This policy explains exactly what happens with information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information:

  • No account or sign-up — the app has no login of any kind.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, or any other device data.

Where your numbers live

Everything you enter — wages, hours, W-4 details, deductions — is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network use

Once a day the app may download an updated tax-rate table (a static JSON file) from GitHub Pages. This request contains no personal data and no identifiers. Like any web server, the hosting provider (GitHub) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses; we never see or use them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app does not target children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting any data (for example, optional purchases through the App Store), this policy will be updated first, and the "Effective" date above will change.
